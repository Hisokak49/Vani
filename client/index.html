Artist Statement

"Art is the quiet courage of embracing the unfiltered self."

Vani Karaikta (@unfiltered_vaniii) is a visual artist and content creator focusing on tactile earth textures, organic minerals, and rich atmospheric light.

Working with raw pigments, oils, and cold wax, each piece is built through intuitive layering and scraping. Her social channels offer collectors and enthusiasts a transparent, unfiltered window into the studio journey.
