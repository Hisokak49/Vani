Common Questions

Commission FAQ

How long does a commission take?

Usually 4–6 weeks from initial concept approval to final varnish and drying.

How is payment handled?

50% upon contract signing & sketch confirmation, and remaining 50% upon completion prior to insured dispatch.

Do pieces come framed?

All canvases come ready to hang with finished edges. Bespoke raw oak or walnut floating frames can be added upon request.
