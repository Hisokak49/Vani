Artist Statement & Bio

"Art is the quiet courage of embracing the unfiltered self."

Vani Karaikta is a visual artist known across social platforms and private collections as @unfiltered_vaniii. Her work occupies the fertile territory between organic earth textures, intuitive mark-making, and rich atmospheric light.

Working primarily with oils, cold wax, and raw earth pigments, Vani builds canvases through a tactile process of layering, scraping, and glazing. Each piece serves as an invitation to slow down, disconnect from digital noise, and contemplate the raw honesty of natural landscapes and emotional states.

The Studio Practice

Mediums & Creative Process

Crafted with archival materials designed to last generations.

Archival Oils & Glazes

Artist-grade pigments ground in pure linseed and walnut oils, layered over weeks to achieve luminous translucency and profound depth of field.

Mineral & Earth Pigments

Raw volcanic ash, natural ochres, and ground slate incorporated directly into cold wax mediums to bring tactile sculptural geology into each surface.

Linen & Belgian Supports

Custom heavy-duty kiln-dried stretcher bars surfaced with hand-primed Belgian linen, ensuring dimensional stability and lasting preservation.
